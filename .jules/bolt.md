## 2025-02-18 - Replacing Synchronous sleep in Home Assistant Entity Methods
**Learning:** Found a performance bottleneck where a Home Assistant `CoverEntity` used `time.sleep()` which blocks the executor threads. In Home Assistant integrations, `CoverEntity` methods should prefer their `async_` prefixed counterparts (like `async_set_cover_tilt_position`) coupled with `await asyncio.sleep()` for yielding back to the event loop. Also discovered that recent Home Assistant test mocks require `discovery_keys={}` for `ConfigEntry` instantiation.
**Action:** Next time I work on Home Assistant integrations, I will immediately check for `time.sleep()` usages and ensure they are converted to async operations. I'll also ensure test mocks include `discovery_keys` if I encounter `TypeError` on `ConfigEntry` creation.
